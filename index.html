Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to complete Volume 1 before using Volume 2?

Volume 2 assumes you know A1-level Portuguese (present tense, basic vocabulary, pronouns). If you've completed Volume 1 or similar A1 materials, you're ready for Volume 2. If you're a complete beginner, start with Volume 1.

I struggle with past tenses. Will this book help?

Absolutely! Volume 2 focuses heavily on past tenses with progressive exercises that help you understand when to use pretérito perfeito vs. imperfeito. The practice is designed specifically to overcome this common challenge.

What's the difference between printed and digital versions?

The content is identical. The printed version is a physical book you can write in. The digital version (e-book) can be accessed on any device for immediate use. Choose based on your learning preference!

Can I use this book for self-study?

Yes! The complete answer key lets you study independently and check your progress. It's perfect for self-learners and also works great as supplementary material for classroom use.

How long does it take to complete?

The book is designed for 9 weeks at one exercise per day. However, you can adjust the pace to fit your schedule. Consistent practice is more important than speed!

I'm at B1 level. Is this book too easy for me?

If you struggle with past tenses or want to solidify your A2 foundation, this book is valuable. If you're comfortable with all past and future tenses, you might want more advanced materials. The exercises are great for review and reinforcement.
